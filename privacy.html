Personal GL Backups

Privacy policy

This utility is operated by its owner for personal backups, not as a public service.

Last updated: October 4, 2026.

Data accessed and purpose

The utility reads the owner's local database to create a consistent snapshot. Through the owner's Google authorization, it accesses backup-file contents and metadata in Google Drive to upload encrypted snapshots, list backups, and download them for integrity checks and recovery.

The Google Drive scope permits reading, creating, modifying, and deleting Drive files. The utility is configured to use a designated backup folder; this configuration does not technically restrict the authorization to that folder. No Gmail, Calendar, or other Google-service access is requested.

Storage and protection

Snapshot contents are encrypted locally before transfer to Google Drive over HTTPS. Encryption keys and OAuth credentials are kept in private configuration on the owner's computers, not on this website or in its public repository. Local database and recovery copies may contain unencrypted data and must be protected by the owner.

Sharing and use

Encrypted backup files are stored with Google Drive at the owner's direction. The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, share it with unrelated recipients, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Access is used only for the backup and recovery functions described here.

The utility's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and owner control

Backup snapshots are retained until the owner removes them or applies an explicitly configured retention policy. The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized operations; it does not delete existing backups. The owner controls deletion of local copies and Drive backup files.

This informational website

These pages contain no tracking scripts, sign-in forms, or upload controls. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process request metadata under GitHub's privacy statement. The website cannot access the owner's database or Google Drive authorization.

Contact

For questions about this personal utility, contact the owner through the site repository. Do not post private data or credentials in public issues.
